🎙️ Fraction Forge Recording Booth

Fourteen short lines — seven for each lesson. Tap Record, read the line, tap Stop, listen back, re-record as often as you like, then Download. Henry hears your voice on that step of the lesson, with the words on screen at the same time.

Please open this page in Safari. Other browsers record a format iPads can't play back. Safari records .m4a, which works everywhere Fraction Forge runs.
Tips: quiet room · hold the phone or iPad about 30 cm away · read it like you'd explain it to him at the kitchen table, not like a newsreader · give the CAPITALISED words a little extra weight, they're the bit that matters · say fractions naturally ("three quarters", never "three slash four") · leave half a second of silence at each end. Skipping one is fine — any line you don't record just stays silent, and the words are still on screen.
When you're done: you'll have fraction-0.m4a … fraction-6.m4a and cubes-0.m4a … cubes-6.m4a in Downloads. Put them in the OneDrive “Fraction Forge Audio” folder (make it if it isn't there) and tell Claude — they get added to the app and work offline from then on.
